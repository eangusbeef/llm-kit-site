How much memory (RAM) does it have?
Not sure? On a Mac: Apple menu, About This Mac. On Windows: press Ctrl+Shift+Esc, click Performance, then Memory. It's the number followed by GB.

Does it have a graphics card (GPU)?
On Windows: Ctrl+Shift+Esc, Performance tab, look for GPU. "Dedicated GPU memory" is the VRAM number that matters. Gamers usually know this one by heart.
